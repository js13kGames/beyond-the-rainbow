🌈 Beyond the rainbow

A powerful curse has stripped a Rainbow Guardian of its magic, turning the once-brilliant unicorn black and scattering the seven colors of its rainbow power across the world.

Now, the cursed unicorn must journey through seven mysterious worlds, each guarded by a powerful guardian. Defeat a guardian, reclaim a colour, and unlock a new ability — from fireballs and dashes to teleportation and time control.

Explore forgotten forests, broken canyons, dark caves, corrupted gardens, flooded ruins, void castles, and the mysterious Time Tower. Each recovered color changes the way you play, allowing you to overcome new obstacles and challenges.

But the journey has one final destination.

After reclaiming all seven colors, the unicorn must face the wizard who caused the curse and destroyed its magic. The wizard has learned to wield the same powers the unicorn fought so hard to recover.

Fight. Explore. Reclaim the colors.

Become the Rainbow again. 🦄🌈**
